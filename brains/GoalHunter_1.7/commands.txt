Allies can order GoalHunter bots:

1) by "Bot Command" pings on a target or tile, or "Caution" on a target or bot to cancel and again to retreat.

2) by chat:

Specific goal:

  <subject name|all|nearby|last> attack|capture|sweep|defend|retreat|cancel <pill#/tank name, defaults to pill>

Ex. "Bot 1 attack 3", "Bot 1 attack Joe", "Bot 1 sweep 4"

General strategic focus:

  <subject> focus <bases|pills|off>

Note: Pillbox repositioning is turned off when human allies are present. Say "repositioning on" to turn it back on.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

More Details:

You can tell GoalHunter bots what to do. There are three ways: a chat line, a
ping on the map, and three shells on one tile.


WHO CAN GIVE AN ORDER

Every player on the bot's team, not only the host and not only the player who
added the bot. The bot checks the SENDER's team, not the chat channel, so a
line in team chat and a line in all chat from a team mate both work. A line
from the other team is ignored, with no answer.


CHAT ORDERS

The shape is:  [who] verb [target]

Examples:
  attack 5             the nearest bot attacks pillbox 5
  all attack 7         every bot on the team attacks pillbox 7
  nearby defend 3      every bot within 10 tiles defends pillbox 3
  socrates attack 5    Socrates attacks pillbox 5, with no auction
  capture base 2       the nearest bot captures base 2
  attack bruce         the nearest bot hunts the tank called Bruce
  retreat              the nearest bot takes cover
  cancel               release the last order you gave

WHO WORDS
  (nothing)   one bot: the one with the cheapest route to the target.
  all         every bot on the team.
  nearby      every bot within 10 tiles of the target.
  last        the bot or bots that took YOUR last order, with no auction, as
              if you had typed their names: "last attack 7". If you have a
              selection running, that is the set instead. With no order
              behind you the bot says "no previous order". "last" cannot
              share the line with a name: that is "last or a name, not both".
  a bot name  that bot, directly. Several names work: socrates plato attack 5.

VERBS
  attack      a live enemy pillbox, or a tank by name.
  capture     a dead pillbox, or an enemy or neutral base.
  sweep       the same as capture.
  defend      one of YOUR pillboxes. It takes a PILL NUMBER only.
              "defend base 3" is turned down: "defend takes a pill".
  retreat     cancel and take cover at once.
  cancel      release an order. See below.
  decoy       not built yet. The bot answers "decoy: not yet".

TARGETS
  A bare number is a pillbox: "attack 5" is pillbox 5.
  A base needs the word: "capture base 2".
  "pill 5", "pillbox 5" and "pills 5" all mean the same.
  A tank is named, not numbered: "attack bruce".

CANCEL
  cancel            release the last order you gave.
  cancel all        clear every live order on the team.
  cancel <bot>      release that bot, as in "cancel socrates".
  Focus and repositioning are settings, not orders. "cancel all" leaves them.

SELECT BY NAME
  One or more bot names ALONE, with no verb, select those bots:
    socrates
    socrates plato
  Each one answers "Awaiting command". Your NEXT order goes to all of them,
  with no auction. The selection ends after about 10 seconds, when you give
  the order, or when you say "nevermind" (or "never mind"). A bot that is busy
  answers "Busy" and is not selected.

NAME MATCHING
  Names are not case sensitive. A shortening works if it is not shared: "soc"
  finds Socrates. A typo within two letters works: "sokrates". Any word of a
  two-word name works: "bruce" finds Bruce Lee. If two bots both fit, the bot
  asks "Socrates or Seneca?" and does nothing. It never guesses.

TEAM SETTINGS
  focus bases    every PILLBOX goal costs the team more, so bases win.
  focus pills    every BASE goal costs more, so pillboxes win.
  focus off      clear it. "take bases" and "take pills" say the same thing.
  reposition on  let the bots move their own pillboxes. It is OFF while a
                 human is on the team. "repositioning on" says the same.
  reposition off stop them moving pillboxes again. "repositioning off" too.
  bot pings on   a bot puts an ATTACK marker on the map every time it starts
                 attacking a pillbox or a tank, so you can see what the bots
                 are going for without reading chat. "botpings on" too.
  bot pings off  stop the attack markers. This is how a game starts.
  These settings take a subject and ignore it: they are team-wide, so
  "socrates focus bases" and "last repositioning on" do what the bare lines do.
  help           the bots say a short version of this page in chat.
  A setting holds for the whole game, or until you change it.


PINGS

Use the BOT COMMAND ping (the purple one in the ping menu). What the bot does
depends on what is under the tile you ping:

  a live enemy pillbox      attack it
  a dead pillbox            capture it
  an enemy or neutral base  capture it
  one of your pillboxes     defend it
  an enemy tank             hunt that tank
  open ground               drive there and hold
  one of your own bots      select that bot
  your own base             nothing at all

TILE RULE
  The exact tile wins first. If something stands on the tile you pinged, that
  is the target, and the eight tiles around it are not searched. Only when the
  exact tile is empty are the eight around it tried, and then the nearest one
  wins.

PING AGAIN TO ADD A BOT
  A second ping on the same target, or inside its 3x3, sends one MORE bot. The
  bots that already hold the order keep it.

SELECT BY PING
  A ping on one of your own bots, or inside its 3x3, selects that bot; it
  answers "Awaiting command". Ping another bot to add it. Your next order,
  whether you ping it, shoot it or type it, goes to all of them.

CAUTION PING
  The caution ping is the cancel. A caution ping in the 3x3 of a target
  releases the bots working on it.
  A caution ping on one of your own bots: if it holds an order, the first
  caution cancels the order and a second one makes it retreat. A bot with no
  order retreats on the first.


THREE SHOTS = COME HERE

Fire three shells that run their full range and land on the same open tile.
Fire all three inside about two seconds, and say it cleanly: no other shot in
the second before the first of them, and no other shot in the second after
the third. The order goes out one second after the third shot, so a fourth
shot in that second takes it back.

The bot with the cheapest route drives there and holds, out of the bots that
were on your screen when the order went out. A bot further away than that
does not hear you.

Forest does not count, and a shell that stops on a wall, a building, a
pillbox, a base or a tank does not count. If no bot is in range, nothing
happens and no bot speaks.


MARKERS THE BOTS PLACE

A bot that takes an order puts an ON MY WAY marker on the place the order
names, at the moment it answers you. On a group order every bot that took it
places one, so the map shows how many are coming. This always happens and has
no setting.

With "bot pings on" a bot also puts an ATTACK marker on a pillbox or a tank
each time it starts attacking it, so you can watch what the bots are going for.
The same bot does not mark the same target again for 30 seconds.

A marker a bot places is news, not an order: the bots read each other's
markers and do nothing about them.


HOW AN ORDER RUNS

An order is a hard order, not a hint. The bots do not weigh the danger on the
map against it. They still defend themselves: they shoot a tank in range, kill
a man beside them, get out of the water and take cover when they are almost
dead. The order comes straight back afterwards.

An order holds for about 60 seconds. Each new ping on the same target starts
the clock again. A bot that runs out of shells on an order that needs them
refuels at your nearest base and comes back; the clock keeps running.

BUSY BOTS
A bot does not take a new order while its man is out of the tank, while it is
finishing a pillbox capture, while it is moving a pillbox it voted to move,
while it is handing itself over to be killed, or while it is escaping water or
a stuck spot. It answers "Busy" and says why.


WHAT THE BOTS SAY

  Got it! attack_pill #5        one bot took it. The wording changes with the
                                bot's name theme.
  Got it! take_cover            a retreat, or a "go there and hold", has no
                                number to say: just the word.
  3 on pill #5                  a group order, said once by one bot.
  Still on it. attack_pill #5   you asked for something it already holds.
  Leaving attack_pill #5 for capture_pill #7
                                a newer order replaced the old one.
  capture_pill #5 done          the job is finished, so the order is over.
                                A bot does not wait out the clock: the tick
                                the pill is yours, or is in somebody's tank,
                                the order ends and the bot picks its own goal
                                again.
  Lost Hannibal                 the tank it was sent after died or has been
                                out of sight for ten seconds.
  Busy (man_out)                it cannot take an order right now.
  Awaiting command              it is selected and waiting.
  Standing by                   the selection ran out.
  Released                      the order was cancelled.
  Refuelling, coming back       a pause in the order, not the end of it.
  Socrates or Seneca?           two bots fit the name you typed.
  didn't understand             the line looked like an order but was not one.

Only lines that start with a verb, a who word or a bot name are read. Anything
else is ordinary talk and gets no answer.


OLD OPERATOR COMMANDS

These are the older debug commands. Every one of them now needs a leading "!",
from players and bots alike, so an ordinary chat word can never freeze a team.

  !stop        pause the bots.
  !start       let them run again.
  !status      the bot prints its current goal.
  !pill:N      go to pillbox N.
  !base:N      go to base N.
  !attack:N    attack pillbox N.
  !cp:N        capture pillbox N.
  !cb:N        capture base N. !cb:all captures every base.
  !bpc:N       attack pillbox N with the blocker-pill test log on.
  !pp:N        place a carried pillbox on pillbox N's square.
  !watch:N     watch pillbox N.

They are hard locks: the bot runs them ahead of everything else, with no
refuelling and no escape. Say "!start" to let it think for itself again.
