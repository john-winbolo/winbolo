BOT COMMANDS ARE EXPERIMENTAL AND IN BETA. Expect rough edges and tell us what breaks.

QUICK INFO:

1) SMART PING

   - Smart ping using BOT COMMAND on a pill, tanks, or land tile to command the bot.
   - Closest bot will respond
   - Keep pinging to add a second bot, etc.
   - Ping "CAUTION" on a target or bot to cancel.
   - Ping "ATTACK" on a pill a bot is attacking to make it charge in now,
     ignoring its own armour, trees and ammo.
     A "CAUTION" ping on the bot or the pill stops the charge.

2) SHOOTING

   - Shoot 3 times in a row to command closest bot to go there for 10 seconds (good for decoys).

3) CHAT

   - Commands (Ctrl-P by default to turn on pill numbers, Ctrl-B for bases;
     Cmd-P and Cmd-B on a Mac)

 Examples:

"attack 5" (closest bot attack pill 5)

"bot5 attack pill 5" (specifically only bot named bot5)

"all attack 5" (all bots attack pill 5)

"4 attack closest" (4 bots closest to the pill closest to the sender)

"4 attack 5" (4 bots closest to pill 5 will attack it)

"last attack bob" (last bot previously commanded will attack enemy player
with bob in its name)

A bot's name may be shortened. "closest" is always a PILLBOX: attack and
capture look at the ones not ours, defend at ours.

   - Overall strategy:

"focus pills"
"focus bases"
"focus off" (back to auto)

4) BOT CONFIRMATIONS

"bot pings on" (ping bot goals)
"bot chat on" (bots chat their goals)

Both take "off". Pings start ON, chat starts OFF. "bot chat off" silences
the goal lines only; an answer you are owed still comes back.

NOTE: Bots <= 7 tiles from a human build no wall blockers when attacking a
pill. They make a suicide run only when that human is shooting at the pill.

NOTE: Pillbox repositioning is off when human allies are present. Say
"repositioning on" to turn it back on.

More Details:

You can tell GoalHunter bots what to do. There are three ways: a chat line, a
ping on the map, and three shells on one tile.


WHO CAN GIVE AN ORDER

Every player on the bot's team, not only the host and not only the player who
added the bot. The bot checks the SENDER's team, not the chat channel, so a
line in team chat and a line in all chat from a team mate both work. A line
from the other team is ignored, with no answer.


CHAT ORDERS

The shape is:  [who] verb [target]

Examples:
  attack 5             the nearest bot attacks pillbox 5
  all attack 7         every bot on the team attacks pillbox 7
  nearby defend 3      every bot within 10 tiles defends pillbox 3
  socrates attack 5    Socrates attacks pillbox 5, with no auction
  capture base 2       the nearest bot captures base 2
  attack bruce         the nearest bot hunts the tank called Bruce
  retreat              the nearest bot takes cover
  cancel               release the last order you gave

WHO WORDS
  (nothing)   one bot: the one with the cheapest route to the target.
  all         every bot on the team.
  nearby      every bot within 10 tiles of the target.
  a number    that many bots, 1 to 16: "3 attack 7". The cheapest routes
              win, so they are the ones nearest the TARGET. Said about a
              running order, it adds bots to that one.
  last        the bot or bots that took YOUR last order, with no auction, as
              if you had typed their names: "last attack 7". If you have a
              selection running, that is the set instead. With no order
              behind you the bot says "no previous order". "last" cannot
              share the line with a name: that is "last or a name, not both".
  a bot name  that bot, directly. Several names work: socrates plato attack 5.

VERBS
  attack      a live enemy pillbox, or an ENEMY tank by name. A team mate's
              name is turned down: "Socrates is on our side".
  capture     a dead pillbox, or an enemy or neutral base.
  sweep       the same as capture.
  defend      one of YOUR pillboxes. It takes a PILL NUMBER only.
              "defend base 3" is turned down: "defend takes a pill".
  retreat     cancel and take cover at once.
  cancel      release an order. See below.
  decoy       not built yet. The bot answers "decoy: not yet".

TARGETS
  A bare number is a pillbox: "attack 5" is pillbox 5.
  A base needs the word: "capture base 2".
  "pill 5", "pillbox 5" and "pills 5" all mean the same.
  A tank is named, not numbered: "attack bruce".
  closest     the pillbox nearest YOU, not the one nearest the bot.
              "nearest" is the same word. "closest base" is turned down, and
              a bot that cannot see you says "can't see you".

CANCEL
  A cancelled order is OVER. No other bot picks it up, and neither does the
  one you cancelled: say it again if you want it done.
  cancel            release the last order you gave.
  cancel all        clear every live order on the team.
  cancel <bot>      release that bot, as in "cancel socrates".
  Focus and repositioning are settings, not orders. "cancel all" leaves them.

SELECT BY NAME
  One or more bot names ALONE, with no verb, select those bots:
    socrates
    socrates plato
  Each one answers "Awaiting command". Your NEXT order goes to all of them,
  with no auction. The selection ends after about 10 seconds, when you give
  the order, or when you say "nevermind" (or "never mind"). A bot that is busy
  answers "Busy" and is not selected.

NAME MATCHING
  Names are not case sensitive. A shortening works if it is not shared: "soc"
  finds Socrates. Any word of a two-word name works: "bruce" finds Bruce Lee.
  If two bots both fit, the bot asks "Socrates or Seneca?" and does nothing.
  It never guesses.
  A TYPO within two letters works too -- "sokrates attack 5" -- but only in a
  line that is already an order: there has to be a verb in it, and every word
  in front of that verb has to be a name or a who word. A typo'd name on its
  own is ordinary talk and gets no answer, because otherwise a sentence like
  "cause we should push" reads as an order to the bot called Case.
  VERBS are matched exactly, never by typo. "attak pill 5" is ordinary talk.

TEAM SETTINGS
  focus bases    every PILLBOX goal costs the team more, so bases win.
  focus pills    every BASE goal costs more, so pillboxes win.
  focus off      clear it. "take bases" and "take pills" say the same thing.
  reposition on  let the bots move their own pillboxes. It is OFF while a
                 human is on the team. "repositioning on" says the same.
  reposition off stop them moving pillboxes again. "repositioning off" too.
  bot pings on   a bot puts an ATTACK marker on the map every time it starts
                 attacking a pillbox or a tank, so you can see what the bots
                 are going for without reading chat. "botpings on" too.
                 This is how a game starts.
  bot pings off  stop the attack markers.
  bot chat on    the bots say their goals: "Got it! attack_pill #5".
                 "botchat" is the same word.
  bot chat off   stop those lines. Only the GOAL lines go quiet. This is
                 how a game starts.
  "bot pings" and "bot chat" go back to these starting values every round.
  These settings take a subject and ignore it: they are team-wide, so
  "socrates focus bases" and "last repositioning on" do what the bare lines do.
  help           the bots say a short version of this page in chat.
  A setting holds for the whole game, or until you change it.


STANDING BESIDE A BOT

A bot attacking a pillbox normally builds a wall in front of it first, which
takes trees and time. A bot with a HUMAN team-mate within 7 tiles skips that
and goes straight in. Only the walls change, and a BOT team-mate does not
count: two bots together keep building.


PINGS

Use the BOT COMMAND ping (the purple one in the ping menu). What the bot does
depends on what is under the tile you ping:

  a live enemy pillbox      attack it
  a dead pillbox            capture it
  an enemy or neutral base  capture it
  one of your pillboxes     defend it
  an enemy tank             hunt that tank
  open ground               drive there and hold
  one of your own bots      select that bot
  your own base             nothing at all

TILE RULE
  A PILLBOX OR A BASE HAS TO BE PINGED EXACTLY. They never move, so put the
  marker right on the tile. A ping one tile off a pillbox is not that pillbox:
  it is open ground, which means "go there". That is how you send a bot to a
  square beside a pill.
  TANKS get the eight tiles around the ping as well, because a tank moves
  while the ping is in flight. The exact tile still wins first, and when it is
  empty the nearest tank in the eight around it wins.

PING AGAIN TO ADD A BOT
  A second ping on the same pillbox, base or tank, or inside its 3x3, sends
  one MORE bot. The bots that already hold the order keep it. If no bot is
  free, the nearest bot on another order switches to it (see A BOT KEEPS
  YOUR ORDER); a bot already on this order is never counted twice.
  A second ping on the same open ground does not add a bot. It starts the
  order's clock again, and the bot answers "Still on it". If no bot holds
  that order any more (it died, it let the order go, or no bot was free the
  first time), the second ping sends one bot again, as the first ping did.

A BOT KEEPS YOUR ORDER
  A bot that holds an order from a person does not take a new ping, a new
  three-shot order or a typed order that does not name it. That goes to a
  free bot, however soon or late it comes.
  If no bot is free, for a ping or a typed order the busy bot with the
  cheapest route switches: it drops its old order and says "No free bot.
  Leaving ... for ...". A tie goes to the lower player number. A bot on a
  decoy hold never switches, and a bot that is busy for its own reasons
  (man out, escaping water, and so on) does not either.
  If no bot can switch, nobody goes: one bot says "All bots busy" and the
  order waits for about 60 seconds: the first bot that comes free takes it.
  A three-shot order with no free bot is dropped without a word.
  To move a bot that is working on an order, name it ("socrates attack 5"),
  select it with a ping, use "all" or "nearby", or cancel its order first.

SELECT BY PING
  A ping on one of your own bots, or inside its 3x3, selects that bot; it
  answers "Awaiting command". Ping another bot to add it. Your next order,
  whether you ping it, shoot it or type it, goes to all of them.

CAUTION PING
  The caution ping is the cancel. A caution ping in the 3x3 of a place the
  bots were sent to releases the bots working on it. With no order on that
  place a caution does nothing.
  A caution ping on one of your own bots: if it holds an order, the first
  caution cancels the order and a second one makes it retreat. A bot with no
  order retreats on the first.


THREE SHOTS = COME HERE

Fire three shells that run their full range and land on the same open tile.
Fire all three inside about two seconds, and say it cleanly: no other shot in
the second before the first of them, and no other shot in the second after
the third. The order goes out one second after the third shot, so a fourth
shot in that second takes it back.

The bot with the cheapest route drives there and holds, out of the bots that
were on your screen when the order went out. A bot further away than that
does not hear you, and nor does a bot that is working on an order you gave.

A place order is a hard one. The bot drops what it was doing and drives to the
square. On the way it picks no goals of its own -- no pills, no bases, no
cover. It has about 60 seconds to get there; an order that never arrives runs
out on that.

When it arrives it says "holding 10s" and stands on the square for ten
seconds. During the hold it does not drive anywhere, but it does fight: it
turns on an enemy tank in range and shoots it, and it shoots an enemy man
beside it. Its own builder carries on as usual. After the ten seconds the
order is over, without a word, and the bot picks its own goals again.

You can end it earlier with a caution ping or "cancel", or by giving it
an order that names it. A new ping somewhere else does not end it: that goes
to a free bot. A ping on open ground does the same thing as the three shells.

Forest does not count, and a shell that stops on a wall, a building, a
pillbox, a base or a tank does not count. If no bot is in range, nothing
happens and no bot speaks.


MARKERS THE BOTS PLACE

A bot that takes an order puts an ON MY WAY marker on the place the order
names, at the moment it answers you. On a group order every bot that took it
places one, so the map shows how many are coming. This always happens and has
no setting.

With "bot pings on" a bot also puts an ATTACK marker on a pillbox or a tank
each time it starts attacking it, so you can watch what the bots are going for.
The same bot does not mark the same target again for 30 seconds.

A marker a bot places is news, not an order: the bots read each other's
markers and do nothing about them.


HOW AN ORDER RUNS

An order is a hard order, not a hint. The bots do not weigh the danger on the
map against it. They still defend themselves: they shoot a tank in range, kill
a man beside them, get out of the water and take cover when they are almost
dead. The order comes straight back afterwards.

An order holds for about 60 seconds. Each new ping on the same target starts
the clock again. A bot that runs out of shells on an order that needs them
refuels at your nearest base and comes back; the clock keeps running.

A "go there" order is the one exception: the 60 seconds is the time it has to
GET there, and once it arrives it holds the square for ten seconds and then
lets go. See THREE SHOTS = COME HERE above.

An order ends the moment its job is gone. A defend order on one of your
pillboxes ends as soon as that pillbox is not yours any more -- shot flat, or
taken by the enemy. The bot says "lost pill #5" and goes back to its own
goals, which may well be to go and take it back.

BUSY BOTS
A bot does not take a new order while its man is out of the tank, while it is
finishing a pillbox capture, while it is moving a pillbox it voted to move,
while it is handing itself over to be killed, or while it is escaping water or
a stuck spot. It answers "Busy" and says why.
A bot that works on an order from a person does not take a new order that
does not name it while a free bot can go (see A BOT KEEPS YOUR ORDER). When
no bot is free and none can switch, one bot says "All bots busy". When the
bots only say no because none of them can get there, nothing is said.


WHAT THE BOTS SAY

  Got it! attack_pill #5        one bot took it. The wording changes with the
                                bot's name theme.
  Got it! take_cover            a retreat has no number to say: just the word.
  Got it! goto                  a "go there and hold" (a ping on open ground,
                                or three shells on one square). The bot picks
                                no goals of its own while it is going: that is
                                what the order means.
  holding 10s                   it got to the square. It now stands there for
                                that many seconds, fighting what comes into
                                range but driving nowhere. Nothing is said
                                when the time is up.
  3 on pill #5                  a group order, said once by one bot.
  Still on it. attack_pill #5   you asked for something it already holds.
  All bots busy                 no bot was free for your order and none
                                could switch. It waits about 60 seconds for
                                a bot to come free.
  Leaving attack_pill #5 for capture_pill #7
                                a newer order that names the bot replaced
                                the old one.
  No free bot. Leaving goto for attack_pill #5
                                no bot was free, so this one, the nearest,
                                dropped its old order for your new one.
  capture_pill #5 done          the job is finished, so the order is over.
                                A bot does not wait out the clock: the tick
                                the pill is yours, or is in somebody's tank,
                                the order ends and the bot picks its own goal
                                again.
  lost pill #5                  the pillbox it was defending is not yours any
                                more, so there is nothing left to defend. The
                                order ends and the bot picks its own goals.
  Lost Hannibal                 the tank it was sent after died or has been
                                out of sight for ten seconds.
  Busy (man_out)                it cannot take an order right now.
  Awaiting command              it is selected and waiting.
  Standing by                   the selection ran out.
  Released                      the order was cancelled.
  Refuelling, coming back       a pause in the order, not the end of it.
  Socrates or Seneca?           two bots fit the name you typed.
  X is on our side              you aimed attack at a team mate.
  didn't understand             the line looked like an order but was not one.

Only lines that start with a verb, a who word, a setting word or a bot name
spelled right are read. Anything else is ordinary talk and gets no answer.


OLD OPERATOR COMMANDS

These are the older debug commands. Every one of them now needs a leading "!",
from players and bots alike, so an ordinary chat word can never freeze a team,
and only a team mate may give one: a line from the other side is ignored.
They are read before any of the orders above, so "!attack:5" is this list's
attack and "!attack 5" -- with a space -- is the order.

  !stop        pause the bots.
  !start       let them run again.
  !status      the bot prints its current goal.
  !pill:N      go to pillbox N.
  !base:N      go to base N.
  !attack:N    attack pillbox N.
  !cp:N        capture pillbox N.
  !cb:N        capture base N. !cb:all captures every base.
  !bpc:N       attack pillbox N with the blocker-pill test log on.
  !pp:N        place a carried pillbox on pillbox N's square.
  !watch:N     watch pillbox N.

They are hard locks: the bot runs them ahead of everything else, with no
refuelling and no escape. Say "!start" to let it think for itself again.
