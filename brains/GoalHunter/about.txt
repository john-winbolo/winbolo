This bot hunts enemy pillboxes and bases, coordinates with its allied bots to strike key targets together, keeps its own pillboxes alive, and refuels when it runs low. How hard it plays is a per-bot setting made in the lobby, not a property of the brain: Easy, Medium and Hard all run this same code.
