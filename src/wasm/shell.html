⚠ Beta — known issues
  • Don't background the tab. Switching to another tab or minimizing the window can disconnect you: browsers throttle and sleep background tabs, which drops the live game connection. Keep the game tab focused and in front while playing.
  • The web client has no in-game settings/keybinding screen yet. Bindings saved on your account (desktop client) are applied on load.
